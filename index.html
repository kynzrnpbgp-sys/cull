Cull

Audit your Pinterest saves, one swipe at a time.

Cull is a personal tool for reviewing pins you've saved on Pinterest. Each pin shows up like a card — swipe or tap to keep it or mark it for removal, with a confirmation step before anything is actually deleted. It also scans a board for duplicate saves and lets you clear out the extras in one pass.

KeepDump

What it does

  • Reads your Pinterest boards and pins so you can review them
  • Detects duplicate saves (same image or link) and lets you remove the extras
  • Deletes a pin from Pinterest only after you explicitly confirm

What it doesn't do

  • It's not a public product — it only connects to my own Pinterest account
  • It doesn't share, sell, or store your data anywhere outside your own device
  • It has no analytics or tracking

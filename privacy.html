Privacy Policy — Cull

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Cull is a personal-use application that connects to your own Pinterest account so you can review your saved pins and remove ones you no longer want, including duplicate saves.

Who this covers

Cull is operated by an individual developer for personal use and is not offered as a public product. This policy describes how the app handles data for the Pinterest account(s) that connect to it.

What data Cull accesses

When you connect your Pinterest account, Cull requests access to:

  • Your boards (names and pin counts)
  • Your pins on those boards (image, title/description, link, and save date)

Cull uses this access only to display your pins for review and, when you confirm, to delete a pin you've chosen to remove.

What data Cull stores

  • Access token: stored locally, in a configuration file on the device you run it on. It is not transmitted to any server other than Pinterest's own API.
  • Pin data: loaded into memory to display it, not saved to a database or file, and not sent to any third party.
  • No analytics or tracking: Cull does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services, and does not collect data about how you use it.

What data Cull shares

Cull does not share, sell, or transmit your data to any third party. The only network communication Cull performs is directly between your device and Pinterest's own API, using your token, to read your boards/pins or delete a pin you've approved for removal.

Deleting data

Any pin deletion happens only after you explicitly confirm it in the app, and is sent directly to Pinterest as a normal delete request — the same as deleting a pin from Pinterest itself. Cull keeps no copy of deleted pins.

You can revoke Cull's access at any time from your Pinterest account settings, or by deleting the local access token.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
